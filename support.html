BE THERE FOR SOMEONE

You don't need
all the answers.

Sometimes, the most meaningful thing you can do is simply listen and let someone know they are not alone.

01 — HOW TO HELP

Simple ways to support someone

01

Listen without judging.

Give the person your attention and allow them to express their feelings without immediately criticizing or dismissing them.

02

Ask how they are doing.

A simple and sincere question can show someone that you care about what they are going through.

03

Respect their feelings.

Avoid saying things like "just cheer up." Instead, acknowledge that what they are feeling matters to them.

04

Offer practical support.

Ask what you can do to help rather than assuming what they need.

05

Encourage professional support.

If someone is having a difficult time, encourage them to talk with a trusted adult, counselor, or qualified mental health professional.

♥

Sometimes, being there is enough to make someone feel less alone.

A little kindness can go a long way.

02 — WHAT TO SAY

Start with simple words.

“

I'm here for you.

Let them know they don't have to face everything by themselves.

“

I'm listening.

Give them space to share what they are comfortable sharing.

“

How can I help?

Let the person tell you what kind of support they need.

You can care
without carrying everything alone.

If someone needs more support than you can provide, help them connect with a trusted adult or professional.
