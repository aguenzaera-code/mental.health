YOU DON'T HAVE TO DO IT ALONE

Reaching out
is okay.

Everyone needs support sometimes. Talking with someone you trust can be a meaningful first step.

♥

01 — WHERE TO START

Who can you reach out to?

🏫

School Support

Talk to a teacher, guidance counselor, school nurse, or another trusted school staff member.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family & Trusted People

Consider talking with a parent, guardian, family member, close friend, or another trusted person.

🩺

Mental Health Professionals

Qualified mental health professionals can provide appropriate guidance, assessment, and support.

02 — TAKING THE FIRST STEP

You can start with one sentence.

01

"I've been having a hard time lately."

02

"Can I talk to you about something?"

03

"I think I need some support."

!

IMPORTANT

If you are in immediate danger

Contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, stay with someone you trust and tell them what is happening.

For urgent mental health concerns, seek help from a qualified professional or appropriate crisis service available in your location.

Asking for help
is not a failure.

It is okay to need support. You deserve to be heard, respected, and cared for.
