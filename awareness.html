Mental Health
Awareness

Understanding mental health helps us become more aware of ourselves and more compassionate toward others.

01 — KNOW THE BASICS

What is mental health?

Mental health refers to our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It influences how we think, feel, behave, handle stress, make decisions, and interact with other people.

Just like physical health, mental health can change throughout our lives. Everyone experiences difficult emotions or stressful situations from time to time.

02 — WHY IT MATTERS

Everyone deserves mental health awareness.

🧠

Better Understanding

Learning about mental health can help reduce misunderstandings and encourage empathy.

💬

Open Conversations

Talking about mental health can make it easier for people to express what they are experiencing.

🤝

More Support

Awareness can help people recognize when they or someone else may need additional support.

03 — BE AWARE

Possible signs someone may be struggling

01

Noticeable changes in mood or behavior.

02

Pulling away from friends, family, or activities.

03

Difficulty concentrating or managing everyday responsibilities.

04

Changes in sleeping, eating, or energy levels.

05

Frequently feeling overwhelmed, worried, sad, or emotionally exhausted.

These signs do not automatically mean someone has a mental health condition. They are simply reasons to show care, listen, and consider reaching out for support.

04 — BREAK THE STIGMA

Let's challenge common misconceptions.

MYTH

"Talking about mental health makes things worse."

REALITY

Safe and supportive conversations can help people feel heard and encourage them to seek appropriate help.

MYTH

"Asking for help means you're weak."

REALITY

Reaching out for support is a healthy way of responding when something feels difficult to handle alone.
