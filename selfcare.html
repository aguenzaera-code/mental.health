TAKE CARE OF YOURSELF

Small steps
matter.

Self-care is about taking care of your physical, emotional, and mental well-being.

01 — EVERYDAY SELF-CARE

Simple ways to care for your well-being

😴

Get enough rest

Give yourself time to sleep and recover. Rest is an important part of taking care of yourself.

💧

Stay hydrated

Drinking enough water throughout the day supports your body's everyday needs.

🚶

Move your body

Try walking, stretching, dancing, or another form of movement that you enjoy.

🎨

Enjoy your hobbies

Make time for activities that help you relax, express yourself, or simply have fun.

📵

Take a break

Give yourself permission to step away from stressful situations and screens when needed.

💬

Talk about your feelings

Sharing your feelings with someone you trust can help you feel heard and supported.

REMEMBER

Self-care isn't selfish.

You deserve time to rest, breathe, recharge, and take care of yourself without feeling guilty.

02 — A SIMPLE DAILY CHECK-IN

Ask yourself:

01

How am I feeling?

Take a moment to identify what you are feeling without judging yourself.

02

What do I need?

Maybe you need rest, food, connection, quiet time, or someone to talk to.

03

What's one small step?

Choose one realistic thing you can do to support yourself today.

Be patient with yourself.

You are allowed to grow at your own pace.
